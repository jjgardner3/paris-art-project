Jessica Anne Schwartz

In 2023 Jessica Anne Schwartz made the Luxembourg's Sénat chairs a fixed listening post: her Jardin du Luxembourg Synaesthesia Drawings transcribe the garden's soundscape — children at play, birdsong, wind and rain — in sumi ink, walnut ink, mica and gouache on Murillo paper. Twelve drawings from the series.

Allard van Hoorn

A used Sénat chair, borrowed from the Jardin du Luxembourg itself, suspended and played with earth magnets and a resonator, so that its steel frame becomes a resonating body. The installation is completed by a Manifesto-Score for the chairs as monuments to gathered time.
